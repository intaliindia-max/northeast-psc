About Northeast PSC

Arunachal's own coaching for state exams

Northeast PSC prepares aspirants for APPSC and APSSB exams. Every batch runs on a planned syllabus with a coordinator who tracks classes and coverage, and every class is recorded so no student falls behind.

Our practice sets come from previous-year papers, and our books cover current affairs and Indian economy with Arunachal-specific content that national coaching brands leave out.

FAQ

Common questions

Which exams do you prepare students for?

APPSC CCE (Prelims and Mains), APSSB CSL and other APSSB exams. We also run foundation batches for beginners.

Can I study online if I don't live near a centre?

Yes. You can join classes live online or watch recordings on our app, and switch to hybrid if you visit a centre sometimes.

If I miss a class, how do I catch up?

Every class is recorded and uploaded to our app under your batch, so you can watch it later.

How do I find out the fee and start date?

Call 60096 07689 or 84150 96728, send us an enquiry on WhatsApp, or visit any centre.
