APSSB Maths & Reasoning Practice by Amarjeet Sir - website files

PUT IT ON NORTHEASTPSC.CO.IN (for the person who manages the website)
1. Open the hosting control panel (for example cPanel) and go to File Manager > public_html.
2. Open the folder maths-by-amarjeet-sir (create it if it is not there) and delete the old placeholder index.html.
3. Upload this zip into that folder, then right-click it and choose Extract.
4. index.html, images, sw.js and manifest.webmanifest must sit directly inside maths-by-amarjeet-sir, not inside another folder.
5. Open https://northeastpsc.co.in/maths-by-amarjeet-sir/ to check.
For an update, extract the new zip the same way and replace the old files. Students' phones pick up the new version on their own.

OR PUT IT ONLINE FREE (on a laptop or computer)
Netlify: open app.netlify.com/drop, sign up free, drag this zip into the box, then change the site name.
Cloudflare Pages: dash.cloudflare.com > Workers & Pages > Create application > Drag and drop your files. Name the project, drop this zip and choose Deploy site.
